Coverage Options

Find the right policy for where you are

Every family's needs look different. Here's a quick guide to the most common types of coverage we help people find.

We write policies for every age — from newborns to 90.

Term Life

Coverage for a fixed period — typically 10, 20, or 30 years. Straightforward and budget-friendly, this is the most common choice for parents covering the years their kids are growing up, or the length of a mortgage.

Whole Life

Permanent coverage that lasts your entire life and builds cash value over time, which you can borrow against. Higher premiums than term, but the coverage never expires and the value grows.

Mortgage Protection

Coverage sized to your mortgage balance, so your family can stay in the home even if something happens to you. Payout typically declines alongside your loan balance, keeping premiums lower.

Index Universal Life

Permanent coverage with flexible premiums and cash value growth tied to a market index — offering more upside potential than whole life, with downside protection built in.

Fixed Annuities

A guaranteed stream of income for retirement, with a fixed interest rate and no exposure to market swings. A steady complement to life insurance for long-term financial security.

Guaranteed Whole Life

Permanent coverage with no medical exam and guaranteed acceptance regardless of health history. Premiums and payout are locked in for life — a common fit for older applicants or those with health conditions.
